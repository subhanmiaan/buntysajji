## Admin guide

Open `/admin/homepage` after signing in:

1. Upload a genuine hero photo or paste its image URL; save to publish.
2. Under **Dining & events**, enter confirmed details, optional capacity/seating information and an optional photo (upload or URL). Enable **Publish confirmed dining and event facilities** when ready. Until then, customers see an enquiry rather than claims about unverified facilities.
3. Under **Genuine customer review**, enter the quote, customer name and public HTTPS source URL, then enable publication. Incomplete reviews cannot be published. HTML in a quote is displayed as text.
4. Use **Menu** for dish descriptions, serving information, portion names, prices and add-ons; **Delivery zones** for fees/minimums/ETAs; **Deals & vouchers** for scheduled offers.

New options live in the existing `restaurant_settings.homepage` JSONB field. Existing records remain compatible. No schema migration or new credentials are needed. Real hosted authentication and image storage retain their existing Supabase integrations.

## Analytics

`POST /api/events` accepts only `menu_click`, `whatsapp_click`, `add_to_cart`, `checkout_started`, `checkout_abandoned` and `order_completed`. `order_completed` means the order was successfully submitted, not that food was delivered or cash collected. Abandonment is a best-effort page-exit signal and is not guaranteed on mobile process termination.

Events emit structured `storefront_event` records to server logs. Names, phone numbers, addresses, notes, URLs, tokens and order IDs are not accepted in event payloads. DNT/GPC prevent collection. This is log-based instrumentation; a retained analytics dashboard/provider is not configured. Count event names in the hosting logs to inspect the funnel.
